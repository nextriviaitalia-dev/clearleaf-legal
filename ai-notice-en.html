Notice on the use of artificial intelligence

Clearleaf can create short summaries of the documents you scan using an artificial intelligence model that runs entirely on your iPhone. Before relying on them, keep these points in mind.

AI can make mistakes

  • It can leave out important information (a deadline, a clause, an amount).
  • It can misread the text or mix up data, names and dates.
  • It can make up details that are not in the document.
  • It works on the text recognized by OCR, which can itself contain errors.

How to use summaries

  • Treat them as a way to get your bearings, not a replacement for the document.
  • Always check any information that matters against the original document: dates, amounts, names, obligations.
  • Do not use them as the sole basis for important decisions.

Not professional advice

Summaries are not legal, tax, accounting, medical or financial advice. For important decisions, consult a professional.

Privacy

Summarizing happens on your device: the content of your documents is not sent to external servers. Generated content is labeled as AI-generated, including when you copy or share it.

Models used

  • Apple system model (Apple Intelligence), if available on your iPhone.
  • Qwen 3.5 4B (Alibaba Cloud, Apache 2.0 license), in the Light (Q4) and Precise (Q8) versions, downloadable from Hugging Face at your request.
