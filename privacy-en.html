Clearleaf Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 — effective from 18 October 2026

In short

  • Clearleaf does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data.
  • Documents, images, recognized text, PDFs and summaries stay on your device.
  • No account, no advertising, no usage analytics, no tracking.
  • The only Internet connection is the optional download of AI models from Hugging Face, which happens only if you ask for it.

1. Owner and contact

Riccardo Fatone, Via Entratico 4, 00188 Rome (RM), Italy. Email: nextriviaitalia@gmail.com. Because the App does not collect personal data on the Developer's servers, the Developer does not process your personal data through the App. This policy still explains in detail what happens to data on your device.

2. Data processed on your device

Data Why Where it stays
Camera images Scanning documents On your device only
Photos and files you import Adding them as pages On your device only
Recognized text (OCR) Search and searchable PDFs On your device only
AI-generated summaries Showing and saving them with the document On your device only
Preferences (theme, settings, terms acceptance) Making the App work the way you chose On your device only

Text recognition and summaries run locally using operating system technologies (Vision, Apple Intelligence) or the model you downloaded. No content from your documents is sent to the Developer or to third parties.

3. Permissions

  • Camera: needed to scan. You can revoke it in Settings › Clearleaf.
  • Photos and Files: the App only accesses the items you pick with the system picker.

4. AI model downloads

If you choose to download a model (for example Qwen 3.5), the App connects to Hugging Face (huggingface.co) to download its files. As with any download, Hugging Face receives technical information such as your IP address and request details, which it handles under its own privacy policy. The App never sends any content of your documents to Hugging Face.

5. Backups and sharing

  • Your documents may be included in device backups (iCloud or computer) according to your system settings. AI models are excluded from backups.
  • When you share a PDF or a summary (Mail, Messages, Files, etc.), the content goes to the app or service you choose, which handles it under its own rules.

6. Bug reports and suggestions

If you use "Bugs and ideas", the App prepares an email to nextriviaitalia@gmail.com with what you write, a reference code, the screenshots you choose to attach and, if you leave the option on, some technical details (App and iOS version, device model, free memory and storage, language, active AI engine, number of documents). The email is only sent when you send it from your mail app. We use this data only to reply and to improve the App (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and keep it as long as needed to handle the report, at most 24 months.

7. Other apps by the developer

The "From the same developer" section is built into the App and does not download data from the Internet. Tapping an app opens its App Store page, operated by Apple.

8. Children

The App is not specifically directed at children and collects no data from any user.

9. Your rights

The Developer holds no data about you, except the emails you choose to send: you can ask to access, correct or delete them by writing to the same address. You can delete your documents in the App or delete the App and all its data at any time. For any question write to nextriviaitalia@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Italy: Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, garanteprivacy.it).

10. Changes

Any changes to this policy will be published in the App and at https://nextriviaitalia-dev.github.io/clearleaf-legal/privacy-en.html, with the update date.
